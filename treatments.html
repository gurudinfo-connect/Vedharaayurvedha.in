Weight Wellness

Weight Loss Treatment

Ayurveda approaches healthy weight management by considering digestion, daily habits, food patterns, activity levels, sleep and individual constitution. Our Weight Loss Treatment focuses on creating sustainable wellness habits rather than quick or extreme approaches. Depending on your requirements, the programme may combine traditional Ayurvedic therapies, herbal preparations, dietary guidance, lifestyle recommendations and supportive body treatments — personalized after consultation to complement a balanced lifestyle.

Explore Weight Wellness
  • Supports healthy weight-management routines
  • Encourages balanced digestion and metabolism
  • Promotes mindful eating habits
  • Supports an active and balanced lifestyle
  • Encourages long-term wellness habits

Guests wishing to build healthier daily habits, visitors interested in Ayurveda's approach to digestion and metabolism, and anyone looking to complement an active lifestyle with traditional wellness support.

Your programme begins with a detailed consultation covering diet, routine and constitution. From there, our practitioners recommend a personalized combination of therapies, movement and lifestyle guidance to support your goals.

Results vary by individual. This programme is designed to support a balanced lifestyle and does not guarantee specific weight-loss outcomes.

Relaxation

Relaxation Treatment

Relaxation Treatment is created for guests who simply need time to slow down, breathe and reconnect with themselves. Traditional Ayurvedic massage techniques, warm herbal oils, gentle therapies and calming rituals are combined to create a deeply peaceful experience. From the moment the treatment begins, the focus is on comfort, tranquillity and creating space away from the pressures of everyday life.

Explore Relaxation
  • Encourages deep relaxation
  • Helps ease everyday physical tension
  • Supports mental calmness
  • Promotes a sense of comfort and wellbeing
  • Creates a peaceful break from daily routines

Guests seeking pure relaxation, travellers wanting a peaceful pause during their stay, and anyone wishing to experience traditional Ayurvedic touch without a structured therapeutic programme.

A soothing full-body massage using warm herbal oils, performed in a calm, private setting. Sessions are unhurried, allowing you to fully relax from start to finish.
