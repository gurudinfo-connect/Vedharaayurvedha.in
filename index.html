Understanding Ayurveda

The Wisdom of Ayurveda

Ayurveda is an ancient holistic system of wellness that looks at balance between body, mind and environment — shaped by individual constitution, food, lifestyle and natural therapies for lifelong, preventive wellbeing.

Vata

The energy of movement — governing breath, circulation and the nervous system. Balanced Vata brings creativity and calm.

Pitta

The energy of transformation — governing digestion and metabolism. Balanced Pitta brings clarity and focus.

Kapha

The energy of structure — governing stability and immunity. Balanced Kapha brings strength and steadiness.
