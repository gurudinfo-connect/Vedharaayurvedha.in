Yoga & Meditation

Movement That Complements Your Healing

Vedhara Yoga brings together gentle asana practice, breathwork and meditation, rooted in the same traditional wisdom that guides our Ayurvedic therapies. Sessions are designed to work alongside your treatments, helping the body stay supple and the mind stay quiet — suited to every level, from complete beginners to experienced practitioners.

Styles & Techniques

What You'll Practice

Every session is shaped around how you're feeling that day, not a fixed routine. Over the course of your stay, guests are gently introduced to a mix of styles — nothing forced, everything adapted to your body and energy.

  • Hatha & gentle asana practice
  • Restorative and Yin-style stretching
  • Pranayama & breathwork
  • Guided meditation & mindfulness
  • Chair-adapted yoga for limited mobility
  • Sunrise and sunset practice by the water

What Guests Take Away

What You'll Learn From Vedhara Yoga

Beyond the sessions themselves, guests leave with tools they can keep using long after their stay — simple, practical, and rooted in the same Ayurvedic principles guiding their treatment.

  • Breathing techniques to calm the mind
  • A short home practice you can keep up
  • Better body awareness and posture
  • Tools to manage everyday stress
  • Habits that support deeper, restful sleep
  • A mindful approach to daily movement

Wellness Focus

What Yoga at Vedhara Supports

  • Encourages flexibility and gentle strength
  • Supports calm, steady breathing
  • Promotes mental clarity and focus
  • Complements your Ayurvedic treatment plan
  • Encourages a mindful daily routine

Good to Know

Is Vedhara Yoga Right for You?

Guests seeking gentle movement alongside their therapies, visitors new to yoga, and experienced practitioners wanting a slower, more mindful practice during their stay.

Sessions are led by an experienced instructor and adapted to your comfort and ability. Expect gentle asanas, guided breathwork and quiet moments of meditation, practiced at an unhurried pace.

Yoga sessions are offered for general wellness and are not a substitute for medical advice or treatment.
