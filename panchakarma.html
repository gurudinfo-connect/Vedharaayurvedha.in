What is Panchakarma

A Classical Path to Deep Renewal

Panchakarma is one of Ayurveda's most comprehensive traditional approaches to cleansing and restoring balance. At Vedhara, each Panchakarma programme is thoughtfully planned according to your constitution, lifestyle and wellness needs.

The experience may include traditional Ayurvedic therapies such as Abhyanga, Swedana, Shirodhara, herbal applications and other classical procedures selected by our Ayurvedic practitioner. Our focus is on creating a calm environment where the body and mind can rest, restore and reconnect with a balanced routine.

  • Supports traditional Ayurvedic cleansing practices
  • Encourages relaxation and restoration
  • Supports healthy digestion and daily routine
  • Helps promote a sense of lightness and vitality
  • Supports overall mind-body balance

Panchakarma programmes are individually planned after consultation with an Ayurvedic practitioner.

Good to Know

Is Panchakarma Right for You?

Panchakarma is often chosen by guests seeking traditional Ayurvedic cleansing, visitors wanting a deeper reset from daily life, and anyone curious about Kerala's classical wellness heritage. It is best suited to those who can dedicate time to a slower, guided programme.

Your journey begins with a consultation with our Ayurvedic practitioner, followed by a personalized sequence of therapies, herbal preparations and guided rest. Each day is paced gently, allowing your body time to respond and restore between sessions.
