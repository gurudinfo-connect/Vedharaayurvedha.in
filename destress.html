De-Stress Therapy

A Rhythm Designed to Calm

Modern lifestyles often leave the mind feeling constantly active. Our De-Stress Therapy combines traditional Ayurvedic techniques with a peaceful environment to encourage relaxation and mental calm.

Therapies may include soothing oil massage, Shirodhara, head and neck treatments, herbal applications and other relaxation-focused practices selected according to your individual needs. The experience is designed to provide a quiet pause from daily responsibilities and help you reconnect with a calmer rhythm.

  • Encourages mental relaxation
  • Helps relieve everyday tension
  • Promotes a calm and peaceful state of mind
  • Supports restful sleep
  • Encourages mind-body relaxation

Good to Know

Is De-Stress Therapy Right for You?

De-Stress Therapy is ideal for guests managing a busy or demanding routine, visitors seeking a peaceful pause from daily pressures, and anyone wanting to unwind mind and body together.

Sessions are conducted in a calm, softly lit space with soothing music and warm oils. Your therapist will guide you gently through each stage, with no rush from one step to the next.
